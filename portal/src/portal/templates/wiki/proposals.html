Pending proposals

Changes derived from lower-trust material wait here. So do the changes dreams propose (marked with their dream; a speculative one rests on a conclusion that goes beyond what your notes state). Review them in LibreChat or your AI app (“review the wiki proposals”). Identity merges, two pages about the same person or thing, are the exception: only you accept them, here.

A proposal is not in the wiki until it is accepted. Pages already in the wiki with status draft, waiting for you to check them, are under Drafts.
