Dreams

A dream tidies up the reviewed part of your wiki in the background: it files new chats as drafts, fixes small things, proposes what follows from your notes, and writes the briefing every conversation starts with. Every change it makes is in the wiki's history and can be reverted here; everything else is a proposal for you. How dreams work
