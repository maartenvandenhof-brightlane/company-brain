Google Drive

The brain writes its pages as Markdown files to a folder named Brain. Gemini reads Brain in Google Drive and saves new pages and versions in Brain inbox as files named brain--<subfolder>--<name>.md. The Gem's instructions are in the chat instructions.

Sync the folders

  1. Once: in Google Drive, create the folders Brain and Brain inbox directly under My Drive.
  2. In: in Google Drive, search for brain--, select all results and Download (Drive makes a zip). Drop the zip into data/drive/inbox; Word files (.docx) in it, for the files Gemini made as Google Docs, are fine. The brain applies it within a minute; once the setup page shows them imported, delete those files in Google Drive.
  3. Out: download Brain.zip and unzip it: you get a folder named Brain. In Google Drive, delete the old folder Brain and upload the new one (New → Folder upload).
