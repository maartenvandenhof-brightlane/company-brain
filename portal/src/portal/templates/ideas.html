How ideas work

The assistant reads your wiki, your goals, your open loops, the hot-memory briefing and what the last dream changed, and suggests things worth your attention, each citing the pages it rests on. Four kinds:

Follow-up
a decision or plan with no sign it happened.
Open question
something you left open, with a next step.
Connection
pages that share a person, problem or assumption.
Challenge
an assumption of yours, with the strongest counterargument.
  • When. After every dream; each night at {{ ideas_at }} UTC when no dream ran that day; after you process imported chats. Ideate reads the whole wiki now.
  • Checks. The strong model proposes candidates; each must serve a goal or loop (a challenge may not), cite pages the wiki has, and not rest on inferences alone. The cheap model scores them; only the best few, unlike anything of the last 180 days, are kept.
  • Your answers. Useful ideas are examples to follow; Not useful, Dismiss and Already doing say what not to suggest. Snooze brings an idea back after 7 or 30 days.
  • Cost. Two model calls per run, within the monthly budget for automated calls. They show on the Usage page.
