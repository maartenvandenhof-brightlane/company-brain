# Company Brain

The *Company Brain* is the company's shared memory: clients, projects, people, decisions and ways
of working, captured once, linked together and kept up to date, so anyone can find them later
without asking around.

AI assistants do most of the filing. When something durable comes up in a conversation, the
assistant searches the wiki, updates the right page or creates a new one, cites where the
information came from, and links it to related pages. Every conversation starts from the
session context: [soul](/context/soul.md) (how to answer), [user](/context/user.md) (the company,
its people and clients) and [memory](/context/memory.md) (standing facts).

See [how to use this wiki](/wiki/howtos/using-this-wiki.md) and the [agent rules](/AGENTS.md).
